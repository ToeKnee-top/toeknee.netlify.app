About me

Friend-of-friends' sites developer.

I'm Tony (ToeKnee-top), a developer and student out of Houston, Texas. My bio basically writes itself: "I like making games for my friend's sites." That's the whole thing, and I mean it.

I build slackbots that have opinions, browser games people actually play, and the occasional over-engineered toy project. A lot of my time goes into Hack Club projects — slinging JS, breaking the build, fixing it, shipping anyway.

When I'm not pushing code I'm probably making something that could be described as "a game" and hoping my friends let me put it on their site.
